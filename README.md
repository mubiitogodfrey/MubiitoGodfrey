## About

My leadership is driven by a simple belief: **everyone should be able to use technology safely, confidently and meaningfully.**

I am **Mubiito Godfrey R, PhD**, a Ugandan cybersecurity professional, technology founder and community builder. As Founder of **All Safe Africa Foundation** and an **ETSI Ambassador in Uganda**, I work across cybersecurity awareness, digital safety, responsible technology, youth empowerment and inclusive innovation.

My work connects technical practice with the realities people face online. I am interested in how cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, human rights and public-interest technology can help build safer and more resilient communities—particularly across Africa.

I believe leadership means listening to communities, bringing people together and taking practical steps to solve problems that matter.

## Leadership, Initiatives & Community Impact

### All Safe Africa Foundation
I lead the Foundation's work to make digital safety knowledge and responsible technology practices more accessible to children, young people, families and communities.

Our focus includes:
- Cybersecurity awareness and digital safety.
- Child online protection and children's rights.
- Responsible technology use and digital wellbeing.
- Youth empowerment, digital inclusion and community learning.
- Partnerships, research and policy engagement on emerging technology risks.

Learn more: [All Safe Africa Foundation](https://allsafeafrica.lovable.app/)

### ETSI Ambassador — Uganda
I support engagement around technology standards and the importance of inclusive, responsible and trustworthy digital development.

### Africa Cyber Resilience Initiative (ACRI2030)
I have been involved in ACRI2030, supporting a broader vision for stronger cyber resilience, awareness, capacity building and collaboration across African communities and institutions.

### CyberSafe Rwenzori 2026
A community initiative connecting digital safety with **human rights, gender equality and community empowerment**. Its focus is to make digital spaces safer while supporting the rights and participation of women, girls and young people.

### Mental Health Tooro Run & All Safe on Phone
These initiatives connect responsible phone use and digital habits with mental wellbeing. They reflect my belief that digital safety is not only about protecting devices and accounts; it is also about protecting people's wellbeing and relationships with technology.

### GDG Fort Portal Chapter
I support efforts to grow the local technology ecosystem by creating opportunities for young people to learn, collaborate, build and grow through developer communities.
